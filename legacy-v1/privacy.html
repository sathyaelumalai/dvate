Privacy policy

Last updated November 2, 2026.

This policy explains what information DVATE collects through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you send a service request or email us, we receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, facility, role, email address, phone number, location and the services you are interested in.

This website is not intended for patient health information. Please do not send patient names, diagnoses or other protected health information through the website form or by regular email. Our clinical team will collect order details through a secure process.

How we use it

  • To respond to your request and contact you about our services
  • To schedule visits and education sessions you ask for
  • To improve this website and our communications

We do not sell your information.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run this website and respond to you, such as email and form providers, or when the law requires it.

Cookies and analytics

This website may use basic analytics to understand how visitors use it. These tools may set cookies. You can block cookies in your browser settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to Amisha Baker at amisha.b@mschealthcare.org or (772) 867-4818.
