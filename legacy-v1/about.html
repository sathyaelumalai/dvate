Distinguished Vascular Access, Transfusion and Education

DVATE, said like deviate, is a promise to depart from ordinary bedside care and set a higher standard for vascular access, transfusion and nurse education.

Our mission

To DVATE from the ordinary and be the leading force in setting new standards in vascular access, transfusion and education: ensuring every patient receives the right access, the right way, the first time.

Our vision

To revolutionize vascular access, expand the boundaries of transfusion care and empower nurses through advanced education, so that DVATE-ing from the ordinary becomes the new standard in patient care.

Our culture

We innovate with precision, where every action is intentional and clinically excellent. Our culture is built on mastery, accuracy and a relentless commitment to continuous improvement.

Because excellence is our standard.

Our team values

  • Excellence

    Every action is deliberate and rooted in a commitment to doing things right the first time.

  • Mastery

    We pursue deep expertise, and growth is our default setting.

  • Improvement

    We embrace feedback, challenge assumptions and evolve constantly, because progress is a habit.

  • Integrity

    We show up with honesty, accountability and respect for our patients, our peers and ourselves.

  • Collaboration

    The best outcomes come from diverse minds working together with shared goals and mutual trust.

  • Transparency

    We communicate openly, share information freely and make decisions with clarity.
