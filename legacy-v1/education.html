Education for the nurses who care for every line

Online learning for your whole staff, hands-on training at your facility, or both. DVATE education builds skills that stay in your building after we leave.

Online education through IV Mastery

A yearly subscription that every nurse on your staff can use.

IV Mastery takes your team through vascular access and infusion care in 17 modules. Each module contains video training and tutorials, competency checklists, clinical posters and a certificate of completion.

When a nurse finishes, a designated preceptor at your facility runs a skills assessment. Nurses who pass earn a competency certificate.

How the online program works

  1. Subscribe for your facility

    One yearly subscription covers all of your staff.

  2. Nurses complete the modules

    Staff work through video training, tutorials and checklists at their own pace and earn a certificate for each module.

  3. Your preceptor assesses skills

    A designated preceptor at your facility observes each nurse performing the skills.

  4. Nurses earn competency certificates

    Nurses who pass the skills assessment receive a competency certificate for their file.

On-site education

We bring our expertise directly to your facility. On-site sessions combine classroom instruction with practical, hands-on training, and we tailor the content to your operations, your devices and the gaps your team wants to close.
