Who we serve

DVATE works with the facilities and providers who care for residents and patients outside the hospital, and who want to keep them there.

Skilled nursing and long-term care

Keep residents in-house for lines, blood and IV therapy

Skilled nursing facilities send residents out for PICC placement, transfusions and IV starts more often than they need to. Each transfer costs money, risks infection and can count against your quality measures. DVATE brings those procedures to the resident's room.

  • PICC, midline and ultrasound-guided IV placement
  • Non-emergent bedside transfusions
  • IV antibiotics, hydration and iron infusions
  • Staff education through IV Mastery and on-site training

Assisted living and memory care

Calm, in-room care for residents who struggle with change

For residents with dementia, a trip to the hospital can be frightening and disorienting. A DVATE nurse can place an IV, run hydration or draw labs in the resident's own room with familiar staff close by.

  • Ultrasound-guided IVs and blood draws
  • Hydration and electrolyte support
  • Consultations on the right device for each resident

Long-term acute care and inpatient rehab

Extra hands for difficult access and device problems

Your patients often arrive with central lines and complex IV regimens. DVATE supports your team with difficult placements, de-clotting, dressing care and removals.

  • Difficult access support under ultrasound
  • De-clotting, dressing changes and removals
  • Port access and maintenance

Home health and hospice agencies

A bedside partner for your patients' orders

When a patient needs a line, a transfusion or an infusion that your field staff do not provide, DVATE can be your bedside partner.

  • PICC and midline placement
  • Infusion therapy and blood draws
  • Coordination with the ordering provider

Physician groups and clinics

Get your patients' orders carried out at the bedside

Physicians and nurse practitioners who round in long-term care can send orders straight to DVATE, so a needed transfusion or infusion does not turn into a hospital admission.

  • Transfusion and infusion therapy
  • Vascular access consultations
  • Documentation back to the ordering provider
