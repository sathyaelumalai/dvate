Bedside transfusions, without the hospital trip

Non-emergent blood and blood component transfusions performed in your facility by trained DVATE infusion nurses.

Why bring the transfusion to the resident

For a frail or elderly resident, a routine transfusion often means an ambulance ride, hours in a hospital chair or an overnight admission, and a trip back. Each step adds cost, exposure to infection and stress for the resident and family.

DVATE brings the transfusion to the resident's bed. The resident stays in a familiar room, your facility avoids the transport and the admission, and the transfusion costs less than it would in a hospital.

A good fit for

  • Residents with chronic anemia who need scheduled transfusions
  • Residents whose physician has ordered a non-emergent transfusion
  • Facilities that want to cut hospital transfers and readmissions

Not for emergencies. A resident with active bleeding or unstable vital signs needs emergency care. Call 911.

What happens during a bedside transfusion

Each transfusion follows a fixed sequence so nothing is skipped.

  1. Order and preparation

    We confirm the physician order, the blood product and the resident's pre-transfusion labs with your team.

  2. Pre-transfusion checks

    The nurse verifies the resident's identity and the product at the bedside and records baseline vital signs.

  3. Transfusion and monitoring

    The nurse stays with the resident, watching closely for any sign of a reaction from start to finish.

  4. Post-transfusion care and records

    We record post-transfusion vital signs, document everything for your chart and brief your nurses on what to watch for.

Common questions

Who performs the transfusion?

Trained DVATE infusion nurses perform every transfusion at the bedside and remain with the resident throughout.

Do we need IV access first?

The resident needs suitable vascular access. If they do not have it, DVATE can place an ultrasound-guided IV or other device during the same visit.

Which blood products do you transfuse?

We perform non-emergent transfusions of blood and blood components as ordered by the resident's physician. Contact our clinical team to talk through a specific order.
