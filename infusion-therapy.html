Advanced infusion therapies

Delivered in coordination with your care team to support recovery and chronic disease management, without sending residents out.

IV antibiotics and antivirals

For infections that need IV treatment

Many residents finishing a course of IV antibiotics can complete it where they live. We start and manage the infusion and the access device that carries it.

  • Start of therapy and ongoing administration
  • Line placement in the same visit when needed
  • Coordination with the ordering provider

Iron infusions

For iron-deficiency anemia

Iron infusions restore iron faster than pills for residents who cannot absorb or tolerate oral iron. Our nurses administer the infusion and monitor the resident throughout.

  • Administration per physician order
  • Monitoring during and after the infusion
  • Documentation for your chart

IVIG and SCIG therapy

For immune deficiencies and certain neurologic and autoimmune conditions

Immune globulin can be given intravenously (IVIG) or under the skin (SCIG). Both need careful rate control and monitoring, which our infusion nurses handle at the bedside.

  • IV and subcutaneous administration
  • Rate titration and close monitoring
  • Teaching for staff on what to watch for

Hydration and electrolyte support

For dehydration and electrolyte imbalance

Treating dehydration early at the bedside can keep a resident out of the emergency room. We place access and run fluids and electrolyte replacement as ordered.

  • IV fluids per order
  • Electrolyte replacement
  • Ultrasound-guided access for difficult veins

Anti-inflammatory and steroid infusions

For inflammatory conditions and flares

Some conditions call for IV steroids or other anti-inflammatory medications. We administer them in the resident's room under the physician's order.

  • Administration and monitoring
  • Coordination with the care team
  • Documentation for your chart

Wellness IV therapy

Hydration and vitamin infusions

Hydration and vitamin infusions administered by licensed nurses, for clients and settings where they are appropriate and ordered.

  • Administered by licensed nurses
  • Screening before each infusion
  • Available by arrangement

Working with your care team

DVATE works from the order written by the resident's physician or provider, and we keep your nurses, the ordering provider and your pharmacy partner informed at each step.

What we coordinate

  • The order, the medication and the schedule
  • The right access device for the length of therapy
  • Monitoring, documentation and hand-off to your nursing staff

Keep infusion therapy in-house.

Tell us which therapies your residents need. We will build a schedule around your team.
